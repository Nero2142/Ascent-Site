Ascent

A daily practice to stay awake, live from your aim, and keep climbing.

Most of us live on autopilot. Ascent is a daily practice for waking up, built on a framework drawn from Gurdjieff's Fourth Way, the Hermetic teachings, Neville Goddard and Florence Scovel Shinn.

Ascent is a reminder: to stay awake, to keep working, to keep improving, to keep going.

Set your intentions each morning, observe yourself through the day, reflect each evening, and climb the Path from leaden sleep to golden light.

Ascent is available on Android through Google Play.
