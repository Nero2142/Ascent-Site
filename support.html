If you are in crisis

If you are in danger or thinking about ending your life, please reach out now. You don't have to go through this alone.

  • Emergency: call your local emergency number (112 in India and Europe, 911 in the US and Canada).
  • India: Tele-MANAS, free and 24 hours: 14416 or 1-800-891-4416
  • United States and Canada: call or text 988
  • UK and Ireland: Samaritans: 116 123
  • Anywhere else: findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines in your country.

Ascent is a practice for self-reflection. It is not a substitute for professional care.

Help with Ascent

Questions, problems or ideas: email paradoxislabs@proton.me. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I cancel Premium?
Open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and choose Ascent.

I'm not getting reminders.
Check that notifications are allowed for Ascent in your phone's settings, and that battery saving isn't stopping the app.

Did I lose my progress when I missed days?
No. Missed days never take away your stage or the seals you've earned. You simply begin again.

How do I delete my account?
See the account deletion page.
