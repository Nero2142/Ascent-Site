Ascent

Privacy Policy

Updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Ascent app ("Ascent", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. Ascent is made by Paradoxis Labs, an individual developer based in India. If you have any questions, email paradoxislabs@proton.me.

The short version

  • We collect only what Ascent needs to work: your account and what you write in the app.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising trackers.
  • Your Aim is never shown in notifications.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

What we collect

Your account. The email address you sign up with, and an account ID created for you.

What you write and do in Ascent. Your intentions, observations, evening reflections, Aims, the Bridge of Incidents, milestones, Honest Conversations, Threshold reviews, your quiz result, your stage on the Path, the achievements you earn, which lessons you have read, and your settings (such as reminder times and when your day starts).

Purchases. If you subscribe to Ascent Premium, Google Play handles the payment. We receive a record of your subscription (for example, whether it is active and when it renews), not your card details.

Crash reports. If Ascent crashes or hits an error, a report is sent so we can fix it. It includes technical details such as your phone model, Android version, app version and what the app was doing, along with your account ID. It never includes what you write in the app.

Reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Their timing stays on your device and in your settings.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising ID.

How we use it

  • To run your account and keep your practice in sync across your devices.
  • To show your progress, your Path and your achievements.
  • To check whether you have an active subscription.
  • To answer you if you contact us.
  • To keep Ascent secure, and to find and fix crashes and errors.

We never use what you write to advertise to you, and we never sell it.

Services that help run Ascent

We use a few trusted services that process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: sign-in and secure storage of your account and entries.
  • RevenueCat: managing subscriptions.
  • Sentry: crash and error reports.
  • Google Play: app delivery and payments.

Each has its own privacy policy. Your information may be stored on servers outside your country.

How we protect it

Information is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers. Access to your entries is restricted so that only your account can read them.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your entries and account details are deleted from our systems within 30 days. Subscription records may be kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for billing and legal reasons.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile, then Delete account. Or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Access or correct your information: most of it can be viewed and edited in the app. For anything else, email us.
  • Turn off reminders: in the app settings or your phone's notification settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU's GDPR. Email us and we will help.

Children

Ascent is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Not medical advice

Ascent is a practice for self-reflection and personal growth. It is not medical or mental health treatment. If you are in crisis, see our support page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
